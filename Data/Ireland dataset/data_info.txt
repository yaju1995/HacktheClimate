DATASET COLUMN DEFINITIONS
==========================

This dataset merges three independent data sources:

1. ENTSO‑E (European Network of Transmission System Operators for Electricity)
2. EirGrid Smart Grid Dashboard (Ireland wind generation)
3. Met Éireann (Irish national meteorological service)

Each column below is annotated with its origin.

------------------------------------------------------------
ENTSO‑E DATA COLUMNS  (electricity generation + load)
------------------------------------------------------------

Forecasted Load [forecast-entsoe]
    - ENTSO‑E day‑ahead load forecast for Ireland.

Actual Load [actual-entsoe]
    - ENTSO‑E actual measured system load.

Wind Onshore [forecast-entsoe]
    - ENTSO‑E day‑ahead wind onshore forecast.

Fossil Gas [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Fossil Hard coal [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Fossil Oil [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Fossil Peat [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Hydro Pumped Storage [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Hydro Run-of-river and pondage [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Solar [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Wind Onshore [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
Other [Actual Aggregated] [actual-entsoe]
    - ENTSO‑E actual aggregated generation by fuel type.

Fossil Gas [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
Fossil Hard coal [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
Fossil Oil [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
Fossil Peat [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
Hydro Pumped Storage [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
Hydro Run-of-river and pondage [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
Solar [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
Other [Actual Consumption] [actual-entsoe]
    - ENTSO‑E actual consumption by fuel type (where available).

------------------------------------------------------------
EIRGRID SMART GRID DASHBOARD DATA  (wind generation)
------------------------------------------------------------

ACTUAL WIND (MW)
    - Actual measured wind generation in MW.
    - Source: https://www.smartgriddashboard.com/roi/wind/

FORECAST WIND (MW)
    - Forecasted wind generation in MW.
    - Source: https://www.smartgriddashboard.com/roi/wind/

------------------------------------------------------------
MET ÉIREANN WEATHER DATA  (meteorological observations)
------------------------------------------------------------

irainfall   Rainfall indicator
Precipitation Amount (mm)
    - Rainfall measurements from Met Éireann.

ittt Dry bulb indicator
Air Temperature (C)
    - Dry bulb air temperature.

iwb Wet bulb indicator
Wet Bulb Temperature (C)
    - Wet bulb temperature.

Dew Point Temperature (C)
    - Dew point temperature.

Vapour Pressure (hPa)
    - Atmospheric vapour pressure.

Relative Humidity (%)
    - Humidity percentage.

Mean Sea Level Pressure (hPa)
    - Atmospheric pressure at sea level.

itabspeed wind Speed indicator
Mean Wind Speed (kt)
Mean Wind Speed (km/h)
    - Wind speed in knots and km/h.

itabdir wind dir indicator
Predominant Wind Direction (deg)
    - Wind direction in degrees.

Weekday
    - Day of week (0–6 or name depending on preprocessing).

------------------------------------------------------------
TIMESTAMP COLUMN
------------------------------------------------------------

DateTime
    - Unified timestamp in UTC.
    - All sources aligned to a common timezone.
    - Resampled to hourly resolution.
